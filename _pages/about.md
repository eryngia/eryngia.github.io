I am an MSc Computer Science student at the KTH Royal Institute of Technology in Stockholm, with a BSc in Computer Science and Mathematics from the University of St Andrews. My background combines software engineering with a strong foundation in mathematics, statistics and machine learning.

## Experience and Interests

I have worked as a software engineer in a banking environment, developing services and testing tools for a distributed messaging system. My other work includes building data-driven systems, analysing large datasets, and designing full-stack web applications. My undergraduate thesis explored adaptive procedural generation, applying mathematical algorithms to produce engaging user experiences, and I have also led a development team in graphics and video game projects.

I am particularly interested in data-intensive and distributed systems, quantitative and financial applications, and graphics and game development. I work mainly in Java, Scala, Python and C++, and I value collaboration within interdisciplinary teams. Further details of my work can be found in the [Education](/education), [Experience](/experience), and [Projects](/projects) pages, or a concise version on the [Resume](/resume) page.
