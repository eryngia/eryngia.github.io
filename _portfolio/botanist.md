The Botanist is an expansion for the narrative card game Cultist Simulator, released on Steam Workshop after more than a year of development. It adds a new legacy to the game centred around a small plant shop with an extensive set of new mechanics. I was the sole programmer and writer, and I also contributed part of the art. I led a team of three artists, three playtesters and a translator, working with them over Discord. The mod has over 5,700 subscribers, with 456 favourites and 15 community awards. It reached the top ten of the game's most popular mods by rating.

Most of the implementation was written in the custom JSON framework of the game, however I also developed Python scripts to automate card and recipe generation with reusable rules. Since the mod changes many existing parts of the game, compatibility between game systems and other important mods such as Hawthorn were a major concern. Furthermore, the writing involved extensive research into historical and mythological botany, which I used as an inspiration to integrate with Cultist Simulator's broader lore. 
 
I learned a lot from managing an entire interdisciplinary development team for this project. I commissioned and directed the art so that the new cards and illustrations matched the game's established style and formed a cohesive visual language. The testers received regular builds and reported through a shared issue board, and I sorted their findings by severity so that the most disruptive problems were fixed first. The translator worked from exported data files, which allowed the mod to launch in English and Chinese at the same time. I continue to maintain the project with new bug fixes and responses to user suggestions.

View the [mod on Steam](https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=2982381815).
