I joined the computer science department at short notice after a teacher of Year 10 and Year 11 GCSE classes left abruptly in the middle of the course. The department needed someone who could support the remaining teacher, since there were more classes than he could teach at once. I had about a week to learn where the classes stood before my first lesson, so I worked through the Edexcel specification and familiarized myself with the Python language.

Together with the head of department, I created lesson plans and exercises for the remaining topics, which included databases, cybersecurity, and coding. The students had very different confidence levels, so each lesson was split into a shared explanation followed by a series of increasingly difficult tasks, with extensions for the strongest students. In the lessons themselves, I helped students with guiding questions instead of fixing the code for them. I also held lunchtime one-on-one revision sessions where we went through a student's work in detail and I helped them improve it.

At the end of the year, the classes all passed their mock exams and GCSEs with a similar average score as the previous years.

**Skills:** Teaching · Planning · Python · Databases · Technical Communication · Time Management
